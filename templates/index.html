FAQ
Tier duoc xac dinh nhu the nao?
Tier duoc xac dinh dua tren hieu suat PvP thuc te trong cac tran danh gia, bao gom combo, di chuyen, timing, va kha nang phan hoi. Moi tran duoc nhieu admin danh gia doc lap.
Toi co the yeu cau xep lai tier khong?
Co. Ban co the gui yeu cau xep lai tier qua Discord. Mot buoi danh gia moi se duoc to chuc de xac nhan tier moi.
He thong co phi khong?
Hoan toan mien phi. AngelTier la he thong phan loai PvP do cong dong quan ly, khong co bat ky chi phi nao.
